About The Centre

Fostering Practical Engineering & Maker Culture

At Government Polytechnic Madhepura, our Centre of Excellence acts as a catalyst for student innovation, bridging theoretical engineering concepts with real-world physical and digital automation.

Our Mission

To equip every student with cutting-edge hands-on proficiency in smart automation (IoT) and rapid prototyping (3D Printing).

IoT Horizon

Connecting physical devices to the cloud, building automated sensor networks, and teaching microcontroller programming under Prof. Nitish Kumar Thakur.

3D Printing Horizon

Transforming computer-aided designs into physical objects with expert mentorship from Prof. Nitish Kumar Thakur, Prof. Surendra Parshad, and Prof. Muntazir Alam.

Core Technological Domains

Our Two Main Horizons

Explore our specialized domains engineered to train students in the technologies of tomorrow.

Horizon 01

Internet of Things (IoT)

The Internet of Things horizon enables students to design intelligent sensor-based systems, smart home automation, and industrial monitoring tools that communicate seamlessly over the cloud.

Microcontroller Programming (ESP32, Arduino, Raspberry Pi)
Cloud Protocols (MQTT, HTTP) & Real-time Dashboards
Sensor Integration (Temperature, Gas, Humidity, Motion)

Domain In-charge

Prof. Nitish Kumar Thakur

Horizon 02

3D Printing & Rapid Prototyping

The 3D Printing horizon empowers students to materialize imaginative concepts into physical models. From mechanical gears to custom enclosures, students learn computer-aided modeling and additive manufacturing.

CAD Modeling & Slicing Software (Fusion 360, Cura)
FDM 3D Printers Operation & Filament Handling
Rapid Prototyping for Final Year Student Projects

Faculty Mentors

Prof. N. K. Thakur, Prof. S. Parshad, Prof. M. Alam
